# Memo — Home Essentials and the GenAI assistant

**To:** Regional Director of Home Insurance; Chief Commercial Officer
**From:** Technical Lead, Direct Digital Channel
**Date:** 30 November 2026

## Home Essentials

We will build Home Essentials **once** and launch it in **Mexico on 30 April, Colombia on 31 May and Chile on 30 June 2027**. Building it once costs about 50 person-weeks instead of 75–90, and the next product will reuse most of it.

To get there, in the first weeks of 2027 we will fix the causes of the 30 September incident, when a problem in Mexico stopped quotes in the three countries. This protects the launches: each country will be able to fail without affecting the others.

**Trade-offs:** Mexico launches first although its technology is the least ready. We accept this with extra controls: a load test before launch and the possibility of switching the product off in one country without affecting the others. The rest of the Mexican journeys will move to the new platform in the second half of 2027.

## GenAI assistant

The answer for Q1 is **no for customers, yes for a test with advisors**. A customer assistant today would need approvals we do not have, and it could give answers that count as regulated advice. In March we will test an assistant for advisors in Mexico for five weeks, and at the end of March you will have a decision with data: if the answers are correct and safe enough, a pilot with customers can start in the second half of 2027.

Meanwhile, Home Essentials will explain coverages in plain language, which addresses part of the abandonment you identified.

## What we need from you

- Your support in the negotiation with our Mexican vendor.
- Support from Compliance and Security for the assistant.
